📖 How to Use Artisan's Ledger

Welcome to the Artisan's Ledger tutorial! This guide will walk you through how to use the core features of your application to manage your RuneScape 3 mining and smithing journey.

🖥️ 1. Overview of the Interface

When you open the application, you'll see a clean, fantasy-styled dashboard split into a few main areas:

  • The Header: Displays your app title, an Autosave Indicator (showing if your changes are saved), a Settings (⚙️) button, and an About (ℹ️) button.
  • The Navigation Tabs: Located right below the header (or at the bottom of the screen on mobile), these let you switch between your Metal Bank, Orders, Item Bank, and the interactive Map.
  • The Action Bar: Floating at the bottom, containing the Save, Level Up!, and Heat buttons.

🪨 2. Managing Ores and Bars in the Metal Bank

The Metal Bank is the heartbeat of your inventory management, allowing you to keep a precise account of your resources.

  • Tracking Your Stock: Navigate to the tracking tabs to view the comprehensive list of available metals and ores.
  • Updating Quantities: Use the input fields next to each item to manually update and keep track of your exact quantities of ores and bars currently in your bank.
  • Depositing Finished Items: Once you have finished crafting your items or completing an order, you can easily deposit the finished pieces directly into your Item Bank.

⚔️ 3. Planning with Work Orders & Material Tracking

Work Orders help you look ahead and map out your smithing plans while letting you know if you are properly equipped.

  • Creating a Plan: Use the Work Order section to map out and make plans for the specific smithing items you want to craft.
  • Material Requirements: The application cross-references your current Metal Bank inventory against your active work order.
  • Status Pills: Depending on your bank supplies, the work order will inform you whether you require materials to complete the order (Need Mats) or if you are good to go (Ready).

🗺️ 4. Using the Interactive Gielinor Map

Need to gather more materials for your next project? The interactive map is built right in.

  • Click on the Map tab.
  • Filter Ores: On the sidebar, click on any ore filter button to highlight its exact locations across Gielinor.
  • Pan and Zoom (Desktop): Click and drag the map board to move around, and use the zoom buttons (+ / -) in the bottom-right corner.
  • Pan and Zoom (Mobile): Use standard touch gestures to pan and pinch-to-zoom.
  • View Region Details: Click on any highlighted region node on the map to open a pop-up window showing a zoomed-in snapshot of the mine and details about what you can gather there.

💾 5. Saving and Backing Up Your Data

Artisan's Ledger automatically saves your progress, but you can also manage your data manually.

  • Autosave: As you type numbers or make changes, look at the top header to see the indicator change to "Saving..." and then back to "All changes saved" with a green dot.
  • Manual Save: Click the Save button at the bottom left to instantly trigger a save animation and lock in all your current values.
  • Exporting Data: Click the Settings (⚙️) button in the top right, then click Export to download a .json file containing your recorded ledger data.
  • Importing Data: Open the Settings (⚙️) menu, click Import, and select your previously saved .json file to restore your progress.

🎉 6. Bonus Features & Settings

  • Level Up Button: Hit the Level Up button at the bottom of the screen anytime you hit a milestone to trigger celebratory on-screen fireworks.
  • Heat Button: Give the forge a stoke anytime for a burst of furnace sound and a warmer glow across the app.
  • Light / Dark Theme: Open the Settings (⚙️) menu and toggle the Appearance switch between Dark Mode and Light Mode to suit your preference.
